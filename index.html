About Me

A developer who builds products, not just code

Hi! I'm Muhammad Asadullah, a Full-Stack Developer based in Lahore, Pakistan with over 5 years of professional experience. I help startups, businesses and agencies turn ideas into fast, secure and scalable products.

My core stack is PHP Laravel, Node.js and Python, with React, Express and MongoDB for modern web apps. I build web applications, SaaS platforms, REST APIs, database solutions, third-party integrations and mobile apps with React Native and Flutter — from database design to the last pixel of the UI.

I'm passionate about AI and automation and help businesses use them to save time and solve real-world problems. I currently work at Techleadz and also take on freelance projects for local and international clients. I value clean code, clear communication and on-time delivery.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to questions clients usually ask before we start.

What services do you offer?

Custom web application development with PHP, Laravel and Node.js, SaaS platforms, mobile apps, REST API development, database design, third-party integrations (payments, CRMs, messaging), and AI-powered automation.

Do you work with international clients?

Yes. I work remotely with clients in Pakistan and internationally, with clear communication and overlapping working hours.

Should I choose Laravel or Node.js for my project?

Laravel is great for business apps, admin panels and SaaS with complex data and fast delivery. Node.js suits highly interactive, real-time apps. I recommend the best fit after understanding your requirements.

Do you build mobile apps?

Yes. I build cross-platform iOS and Android apps with React Native and Flutter, connected to secure backend APIs built with Laravel or Node.js.

How long does a project take?

It depends on scope. A landing page or small integration can take days, while a full web app or SaaS MVP usually takes several weeks. You get a clear timeline before work begins.

Do you provide support after launch?

Yes. I offer bug fixes, maintenance, performance improvements and new features after launch.
