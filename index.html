FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to questions clients usually ask before we start.

What services do you offer?

Custom web application development with Laravel and the MERN stack, SaaS platforms, REST API development, database design, third-party integrations (payments, CRMs, messaging), and AI-powered automation.

Do you work with international clients?

Yes. I work remotely with clients in Pakistan and internationally, with clear communication and overlapping working hours.

Should I choose Laravel or the MERN stack for my project?

Laravel is great for business apps, admin panels and SaaS with complex data and fast delivery. MERN suits highly interactive, real-time apps. I recommend the best fit after understanding your requirements.

How long does a project take?

It depends on scope. A landing page or small integration can take days, while a full web app or SaaS MVP usually takes several weeks. You get a clear timeline before work begins.

Do you provide support after launch?

Yes. I offer bug fixes, maintenance, performance improvements and new features after launch.
